Our Driveway Cleaning Process

We start by applying a specialized degreasing solution to break down oil and grease stains. Understanding Different Driveway Materials

Different driveway materials require different cleaning approaches. Concrete driveways are durable and can handle higher pressure, but they're porous and can absorb oil and stains deeply. We use degreasing agents and hot water to lift oil stains from concrete pores. Asphalt driveways are more delicate — high pressure can damage the surface and cause pitting. We use lower pressure and specialized cleaners for asphalt. Paver driveways require careful cleaning to avoid dislodging the sand between pavers. We use low pressure and may need to replace sand after cleaning. Each material requires specific techniques and cleaning solutions for the best results without damage.

Concrete Sealing: Protect Your Investment

After cleaning your concrete driveway, we recommend applying a quality concrete sealer. Sealer penetrates the concrete pores and creates a protective barrier that repels oil, water, and stains. It makes future cleaning easier — spills wipe up instead of soaking in. It protects against freeze-thaw damage by reducing water absorption. It enhances the color of the concrete and gives it a subtle sheen. A properly sealed driveway needs resealing every 2-3 years. We offer professional sealing services using commercial-grade sealers that provide superior protection compared to hardware store products. Protect your driveway investment with professional sealing.
