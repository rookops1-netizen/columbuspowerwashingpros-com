Why Roof Cleaning Matters

Those dark streaks you see on roofs are actually gloeocapsa magma — a type of algae that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. As the algae grows, it consumes the limestone, causing shingles to deteriorate prematurely. Moss and lichen can lift shingles and create pathways for water to penetrate your roof deck. In Columbus's humid climate, these problems are common and only get worse over time. Professional roof cleaning removes these organisms and can extend the life of your roof by 5-10 years.

Our Soft Washing Process

We use soft washing — a low-pressure cleaning method that relies on specialized cleaning solutions rather than high pressure to clean your roof. High-pressure washing can damage shingles, force water under them, and void your roof warranty. Our soft washing process applies an eco-friendly cleaning solution that kills algae, moss, and lichen at the root, then rinses clean with low-pressure water. The results are immediate and long-lasting — typically 2-3 years before any regrowth appears. We protect your landscaping by rinsing plants before and after treatment.

Types of Roofs We Clean

We clean all types of roofing materials including asphalt shingles, clay tiles, concrete tiles, slate, metal roofs, and flat roofs. Each material requires a specific approach. Asphalt shingles are cleaned with soft washing to avoid granule loss. Tile roofs may require additional care to avoid damaging the tiles. Metal roofs can be cleaned with slightly higher pressure. We assess your roof type and condition before recommending the best cleaning approach.

The Dangers of Algae and Moss on Your Roof

Algae and moss aren't just cosmetic problems — they can cause real damage to your roof. Algae (gloeocapsa magma) feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles, consuming the material that gives shingles their strength and durability. Over time, algae infestation can cause shingles to deteriorate, curl, and lose their protective granules. Moss grows between and under shingles, lifting them and creating pathways for water to penetrate your roof deck. In winter, moss holds moisture against the roof, promoting ice dam formation. Both algae and moss can reduce your roof's lifespan by 5-10 years. Professional cleaning removes these organisms and protects your roof investment.

Copper and Zinc Strips for Moss Prevention

After cleaning your roof, we can install copper or zinc strips along the ridge to prevent future moss and algae growth. When it rains, trace amounts of copper or zinc are released from the strips and wash down the roof, creating an environment where moss and algae cannot grow. This is a proven, environmentally friendly method of preventing regrowth. The strips are virtually invisible once installed and provide protection for 10-20 years. This is an excellent investment for homes with persistent moss or algae problems, especially those in shaded areas where these organisms thrive.
