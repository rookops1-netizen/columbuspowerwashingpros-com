Soft Washing vs. Pressure Washing: What's the Difference?

Many homeowners don't realize there are two different approaches to house washing. Pressure washing uses high-pressure water (1,500-3,000 PSI) to blast away dirt and grime. It's effective for hard surfaces like concrete driveways and sidewalks, but it can damage softer surfaces like wood siding, vinyl, and roof shingles. Soft washing uses low pressure (100-500 PSI) combined with specialized cleaning solutions to kill mold, mildew, and algae at the root and lift dirt without damaging the surface. Soft washing is the preferred method for house washing because it's safe for all siding types and provides longer-lasting results — the cleaning solutions continue working after the rinse to prevent regrowth. Preparing Your Home for House Washing

Before our team arrives to wash your home, there are a few things you can do to prepare. Close all windows and doors tightly. Remove or cover outdoor furniture, grills, potted plants, and decorations near the house. Move vehicles out of the driveway and away from the work area. Cover or disconnect any outdoor electronics or lighting fixtures. If you have pets, plan to keep them inside during the cleaning. Let us know about any areas of concern — loose siding, cracked caulk, or areas where water might enter. We'll take care to protect your property and ensure a thorough, safe cleaning.

How Often Should You Wash Your Home in Columbus?

The frequency of house washing depends on several factors. Homes in shaded areas or near trees tend to grow mold and algae faster and may need annual cleaning. Homes in full sun may only need cleaning every 2-3 years. Homes near busy roads may accumulate more dirt and pollution. Homes with vinyl siding show dirt more readily than brick or fiber cement. As a general rule, we recommend house washing every 1-2 years for most Columbus homes. Regular cleaning not only keeps your home looking its best but also prevents damage from mold, mildew, and algae that can deteriorate siding materials over time.
