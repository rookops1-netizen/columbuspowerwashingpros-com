Our Deck Cleaning Process

We use a gentle but effective cleaning process that removes dirt, mold, mildew, and old finish without damaging the wood fibers. We apply a wood-specific cleaner that lifts embedded grime and kills organic growth. We then rinse using low pressure to avoid damaging the wood. For composite decks, we use specialized cleaners that are safe for the material. After cleaning, we allow the deck to dry completely before applying stain or sealer. We offer a range of stain colors and finishes, from clear sealers that show the natural wood grain to solid stains that provide maximum color and UV protection.

Signs Your Deck Needs Cleaning

How do you know when your deck needs professional cleaning? Look for these signs: the wood has turned gray or dull from weathering, there are green or black spots indicating mold or mildew growth, the wood feels rough or splintery, water no longer beads up on the surface (indicating the sealer has worn off), there are stains from plant debris, bird droppings, or food spills, and the deck looks dirty even after sweeping. If you notice any of these signs, it's time for professional cleaning. Regular cleaning not only restores your deck's appearance but also extends its life by removing damaging organic growth and preparing the wood for protection.

Choosing the Right Stain Color for Your Deck

Choosing the right stain color is an important decision that affects your deck's appearance for years. Clear sealers show the natural wood grain and color but offer the least UV protection. Semi-transparent stains add color while still showing the wood grain — they're the most popular choice for wood decks. Solid stains provide maximum color and UV protection but hide the wood grain. Popular colors in Columbus include natural cedar and redwood tones for a classic look, warm browns and honeys for a traditional appearance, and grays and driftwood colors for a modern, coastal feel. We carry a wide range of colors and can help you choose the perfect one for your home.

Deck Maintenance Between Professional Cleanings

Between professional cleanings, there are steps you can take to maintain your deck. Sweep regularly to remove leaves, dirt, and debris that can trap moisture against the wood. Move furniture and planters periodically to prevent uneven fading and moisture damage. Clean up spills promptly to prevent staining. Trim nearby vegetation to improve air circulation and reduce shade that promotes mold growth. Check for loose boards, popped nails, and other damage after storms. Apply a fresh coat of sealer when water stops beading on the surface. With proper maintenance between professional cleanings, your deck will look beautiful and last longer.

Frequently Asked Questions About Deck Cleaning

How often should I clean and seal my deck?

We recommend cleaning and sealing your deck every 2-3 years. Decks in full sun or near trees may need more frequent maintenance. A simple water test — if water beads up, the sealer is still working; if it soaks in, it's time to reseal.

Can you clean composite decking?

Yes, we clean composite decking using specialized cleaners that are safe for the material. Composite decks don't need staining but benefit from regular cleaning to remove dirt, mold, and mildew.
