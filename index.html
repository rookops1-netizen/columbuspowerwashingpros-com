Frequently Asked Questions About Pressure Washing in Columbus

How much does pressure washing cost in Columbus, OH?

Pressure washing prices in Columbus typically range from $150 to $800 depending on the size of your home and the services needed. House washing averages $200-$400 for a standard single-family home. Driveway cleaning runs $100-$250. We provide free, detailed estimates for every Columbus property. Our pricing is transparent with no hidden fees — the quote we give you is the final price. We also offer package deals when you combine multiple services like house washing and driveway cleaning.

Is pressure washing safe for my siding and roof?

Yes, when done correctly. We use soft washing techniques for delicate surfaces like roofs, siding, and painted wood. Soft washing uses low pressure combined with specialized cleaning solutions to kill mold and algae at the root without damaging your home. For durable surfaces like concrete driveways, we use higher pressure for maximum cleaning power. Our technicians are trained to select the right pressure and technique for every surface, ensuring effective cleaning without any damage to your property.

How often should I have my house pressure washed in Ohio?

We recommend pressure washing your home's exterior once a year in Columbus's climate. Ohio's humid summers and freeze-thaw winters create ideal conditions for mold, mildew, and algae growth. Annual washing protects your paint, siding, and roof while keeping your home looking its best. Some homes with heavy tree coverage may benefit from twice-yearly cleaning. Regular pressure washing also helps maintain your home's value and prevents long-term damage from organic growth and environmental pollutants.

Do you use my water or bring your own?

We bring our own water supply for most jobs. Our trucks carry large water tanks so we don't need to connect to your outdoor spigot. This means no impact on your water bill and no hoses running across your property. For larger commercial jobs, we may connect to a hydrant with proper permits. We also use water reclamation systems when required to capture runoff and protect local waterways, especially when using cleaning agents near storm drains.
