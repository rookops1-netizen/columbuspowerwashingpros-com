Fence Cleaning in Columbus, OH

Your fence frames your property, but over time it collects dirt, mold, mildew, and algae that make it look old and neglected. At Columbus Power Washing Pros, we provide professional fence cleaning services throughout Columbus and Central Ohio. We clean all types of fences — wood, vinyl, aluminum, chain link, and composite — restoring their appearance and extending their life. Whether you're preparing to stain a wood fence or just want your vinyl fence to look like new, our team delivers exceptional results.

Our Fence Cleaning Process

We tailor our cleaning approach to your fence material. For wood fences, we use a gentle cleaning solution that removes dirt, mold, and mildew without damaging the wood fibers. We rinse with low pressure to avoid splintering or damaging the wood. For vinyl and aluminum fences, we can use slightly higher pressure to remove stubborn dirt and grime. For chain link fences, we clean the entire surface including posts and rails. After cleaning, we can apply a wood brightener to restore the natural color of wood fences, or a protective sealer to prevent future growth.

Benefits of Professional Fence Cleaning

Regular fence cleaning removes organic growth that can damage fence materials over time. Mold and mildew can cause wood rot and deterioration. On vinyl fences, algae can create stubborn green stains that are difficult to remove without professional equipment. Clean fences also look better — a clean fence can transform the appearance of your entire property. For wood fences, cleaning is an essential first step before staining or painting, ensuring better adhesion and longer-lasting results.

Fence Staining and Sealing

After cleaning, we offer professional staining and sealing services for wood fences. We use high-quality, UV-resistant stains that protect against sun damage, moisture, and rot. We offer transparent, semi-transparent, and solid stain options. Our sealers provide waterproofing protection that extends the life of your fence. We apply stains by hand or sprayer for even coverage and professional results. A stained and sealed wood fence can look beautiful for 3-5 years between treatments.

Fence Materials and Cleaning Methods

Different fence materials require different cleaning approaches. Wood fences need gentle cleaning to avoid damaging the wood fibers — we use soft washing techniques with specialized wood cleaners. Vinyl fences can handle slightly higher pressure but need care to avoid scratching the surface — we use non-abrasive cleaning solutions and controlled pressure. Aluminum fences are durable and can be cleaned with standard pressure washing techniques. Chain link fences require thorough cleaning of the entire surface including posts, rails, and fabric. Each material has specific requirements, and our team has the experience to clean each type properly without causing damage.

Fence Staining: Protecting Your Investment

After cleaning your wood fence, staining is essential for protecting your investment. A quality stain penetrates the wood and provides protection against UV rays, moisture, and rot. It also enhances the natural beauty of the wood grain. We offer a wide range of stain colors from transparent to solid. Transparent stains show the natural wood color while providing protection. Semi-transparent stains add color while still showing the wood grain. Solid stains provide maximum color and coverage. We apply stains by hand or sprayer for even coverage and professional results. A properly stained fence can last 3-5 years between treatments, protecting your fence and keeping it looking beautiful.

Frequently Asked Questions About Fence Cleaning

How often should I clean my fence?

We recommend cleaning wood fences every 2-3 years, or before staining. Vinyl and aluminum fences may need cleaning every 1-2 years depending on exposure to dirt and organic growth.

Can you remove green algae from vinyl fences?

Yes, we use specialized cleaning solutions that kill and remove algae from vinyl fences without damaging the material. We can restore even heavily stained vinyl fences to like-new condition.
